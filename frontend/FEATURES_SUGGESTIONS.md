# CampusHub – Additional Feature Suggestions

Suggestions for future enhancements beyond the current demo. Use these for evaluation discussion or next-phase development.

---

## 1. **Certificates & Achievements**
- **Download certificate** after completing an event (PDF with event name, date, club logo).
- **Achievement badges** (e.g. “First event”, “10 events”, “Club regular”).
- **Leaderboard** – most active students by events attended or clubs joined.

---

## 2. **Notifications & Reminders**
- **In-app notification center** – list of notifications (new events, registration confirmations, club updates).
- **Email reminders** – e.g. “Event tomorrow: Hackathon 2024”.
- **Browser push notifications** for deadline reminders.

---

## 3. **Calendar & Scheduling**
- **Add to calendar** – “Add to Google Calendar” / “Add to Apple Calendar” for registered events.
- **My calendar view** – calendar page showing only the user’s registered events.
- **Export** – export “My events” as ICS file.

---

## 4. **Search & Discovery**
- **Global search** – search events, clubs, and discussions from the navbar.
- **Filters** – by date range, club, fee (free/paid), type.
- **Recommendations** – “Events you might like” based on interests/clubs.

---

## 5. **Social & Sharing**
- **Share event** – copy link or share to WhatsApp / Twitter / LinkedIn.
- **Invite friends** – “Invite friends to this event” with shareable link.
- **Event check-in** – QR code at venue for attendance (coordinator scans).

---

## 6. **Club Coordinator Features** (when you build coordinator dashboard)
- **Create / edit events** – form with date, capacity, fee, description.
- **Manage registrations** – list of registrants, export CSV, mark attendance.
- **Club analytics** – registrations over time, popular events.
- **Announcements** – post to club feed visible to members.

---

## 7. **Payment & Registration**
- **Razorpay full integration** – backend order creation, webhook for payment confirmation, auto-confirm registration on success.
- **Refund flow** – “Request refund” with reason; coordinator approves (mock or real).
- **Waitlist** – when event is full, “Join waitlist”; notify if a spot opens.

---

## 8. **Profile & Onboarding**
- **Profile photo upload** – replace initials with uploaded image.
- **Interest selection** – during signup or in profile; used for recommendations.
- **Email verification** – verify email before full access (mock or real).

---

## 9. **Discussion Hub**
- **Nested replies** – reply to a specific comment (threaded).
- **Upvote / downvote** – sort by “most upvoted” or “newest”.
- **Pin important discussions** – club coordinators can pin.
- **Report / moderate** – report post; coordinator can hide or remove.

---

## 10. **Mobile & UX**
- **PWA** – install as app, offline view of “My Registrations”.
- **Skeleton loaders** – loading placeholders for feed and events.
- **Infinite scroll** – load more events/feed on scroll.
- **Pull to refresh** – on feed and events list.

---

## 11. **Accessibility & i18n**
- **Keyboard navigation** – full support for tab and Enter.
- **Screen reader** – ARIA labels and semantic HTML.
- **Multi-language** – e.g. English + Hindi (i18n library).

---

## 12. **Admin / Super Admin** (optional)
- **Approve events** – events go “Pending” until admin approves.
- **Manage clubs** – create clubs, assign coordinators.
- **Site settings** – maintenance mode, feature flags.

---

## Priority for Next Phase (suggested order)
1. Razorpay backend integration (order + webhook).
2. Notifications (in-app + email reminders).
3. Certificates (download PDF).
4. Coordinator dashboard (create events, manage registrations).
5. Calendar export and “Add to calendar”.
6. Search and filters.
7. Refund and waitlist.

Use this list in your evaluation or planning doc; you can copy sections into your report or backlog as needed.
